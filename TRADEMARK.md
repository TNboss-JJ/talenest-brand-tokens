## Why both Apache 2.0 + Trademark Policy?

This is the **standard pattern** for commercial open source. Examples:

- **Linux** — code under GPL, "Linux®" is a trademark of Linus Torvalds
- **DHIS2** — code under BSD, "DHIS2®" is a trademark of the University of Oslo
- **Kolibri** — code under MIT, "Kolibri" is a trademark of Learning Equality

The open license invites **technical contribution and adoption**; the trademark policy prevents **brand confusion and dilution**.
